# Battery and charging

## How long does the AR-1 battery last on one charge?

Runtime is up to 90 minutes in Eco mode and about 45 minutes in Turbo mode on
hard floors. Runtime is shorter on carpet because the brush motor draws more
current to keep the roller speed up.

## How long does the AR-1 take to charge?

A full charge from empty takes about 240 minutes. The status ring pulses amber
while charging and turns solid white when the pack is full.

## Does the AR-1 resume cleaning after it recharges?

Yes. If the battery drops below 15% mid-clean, the AR-1 returns to the dock,
charges to 80%, and resumes from where it stopped. Resume only works when the
robot reached the dock on its own; if you carry it back, the map position is
lost and the next run starts fresh.

## What battery does the AR-1 use?

A 3200 mAh lithium-ion pack, part number NW-BATT-32. It is user-replaceable:
four screws on the underside panel, one connector. Expect roughly 500 full
charge cycles before capacity drops below 80% of new.

## Why is my runtime getting shorter?

Three usual causes, in order of likelihood. A clogged filter makes the fan work
harder. A hair-wrapped main roller makes the brush motor work harder. And an
ageing pack simply holds less charge. Clean the filter and roller first; those
two account for most of the runtime complaints we see.

## Can I leave the AR-1 on the dock all the time?

Yes. The charger stops at full and trickles only when the pack self-discharges.
Leaving it docked is better for the battery than storing it off the dock,
because a lithium pack left flat for weeks can drop below its safe voltage.
