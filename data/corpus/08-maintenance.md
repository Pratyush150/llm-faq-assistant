# Maintenance schedule

## What maintenance does the AR-1 need?

Empty the bin after every run, rinse the filter every two weeks, clear the main
roller monthly, and wipe the sensors monthly. Everything else is on failure.

## How do I clean the sensors?

Wipe the four cliff sensor windows on the underside, the two dock-facing
infrared windows on the front, and the clear band around the lidar turret with
a dry microfibre cloth. Do not use solvent; it hazes the plastic and the cliff
sensors start reading false drops, which shows up as E04.

## Can I wash the dust bin?

Yes, the bin body is washable in cold water. The filter is washable separately.
Both must be completely dry before refitting — 24 hours is the safe figure for
the filter.

## How do I store the AR-1 for a long period?

Charge to full, then power the robot off with the switch under the bin, and
store it somewhere dry between 5 and 30 degrees celsius. Charge it to full again
every three months. Storing a lithium pack flat is what kills it.

## What is the expected service life?

Design life is five years of normal domestic use. The consumables — filter,
side brush, main roller, battery — are all user-replaceable, so the practical
limit is usually the drive wheel gearbox.
