AR-1 accessories

Which accessories fit the AR-1?

The auto-empty dock, the extra filter pack (NW-FILT-02), the side brush pack (NW-BRUSH-11), the main roller (NW-ROLL-07), the spare battery (NW-BATT-32) and the carpet-avoid strips. The mop pad and the 180 ml water tank fit the AR-1 Pro only.

What does the auto-empty dock do?

It empties the robot's bin into a sealed bag (NW-BAG-05) at the end of every run. One bag holds roughly 30 bin loads. The auto-empty dock is louder than the standard dock for the eight seconds it runs.

Do the carpet-avoid strips need the app?

No. They are magnetic strips the robot detects with a sensor in the base, so they work with no account and no network. Lay them along the edge of a rug you do not want mopped.

Is the AR-1 Pro water tank compatible with the base AR-1?

No. The base AR-1 has no pump mount and no mop-pad rail, so the tank has nowhere to sit and nothing to feed.
