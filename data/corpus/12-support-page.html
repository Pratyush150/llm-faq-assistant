Contacting Northwind Robotics support

How do I contact support?

Open a ticket from the Support tab in the app, or use the web form on the support site. Include the robot serial number from the label under the dust bin, the firmware version from the app's About screen, and the error code if the robot reported one. Tickets without a serial number take longer because the first reply is always a request for it.

What are support hours?

Tickets are answered Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 local time. First response is normally within one working day.

How do I find my serial number?

Lift the dust bin out. The serial label is on the floor of the bin cavity and starts with NWAR followed by ten digits.

What should I try before opening a ticket?

  • Empty the bin and check the filter is dry and seated.
  • Clear the main roller and the side brush.
  • Wipe the cliff sensors and the lidar band.
  • Restart the robot with the switch under the bin.

Those four steps clear most E01, E02, E03 and E04 reports.

Can I get a replacement dock?

Yes. Docks are sold separately and are covered by the same 24 month warranty as the robot when bought with a unit.
