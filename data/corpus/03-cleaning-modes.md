# Cleaning modes and suction

## What cleaning modes does the AR-1 have?

Three: Eco, Standard and Turbo. Eco is the quietest and longest running.
Standard is the default. Turbo raises fan speed for carpet and pet hair and
roughly halves the runtime.

## How loud is the AR-1?

Measured at one metre on a hard floor: 58 dB in Eco, 62 dB in Standard and
68 dB in Turbo. The dock itself is silent.

## Does the AR-1 work on carpet?

Yes on low-pile carpet up to 20 mm. It detects carpet by wheel current and
raises fan speed automatically unless you are in Eco mode. It will not climb
onto high-pile or shag carpet reliably, and it is not designed for rugs with
long fringes; the fringe wraps the side brush.

## Can the AR-1 mop?

The base AR-1 does not mop. The AR-1 Pro adds a 180 ml water tank and a
vibrating mop pad. Mopping and carpet do not mix: fit the carpet-avoid strips
or set a no-mop zone in the app before running a mop cycle in a room with rugs.

## How do I set spot cleaning?

Hold the button on the robot for three seconds. The AR-1 cleans a 1.5 m square
around its current position and then stops. Spot cleaning does not update the
map.

## What is the dust bin capacity?

0.55 litres on the AR-1 and 0.45 litres on the AR-1 Pro, which gives up some
bin volume to the water tank.
