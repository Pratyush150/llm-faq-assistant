# Warranty and returns

## What warranty comes with the AR-1?

24 months from the date of purchase for the robot and the dock, and 6 months
for the battery pack. Keep the receipt: warranty runs from the purchase date,
not the manufacture date.

## What does the warranty not cover?

Consumables (filter, side brush, main roller, mop pad), damage from liquid
ingress, damage from running the robot over cables or pet mess, and any unit
opened beyond the user-serviceable panels.

## How do I return an AR-1?

Unused units can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Put
the robot, the dock and the adapter back in the original box, request a return
label from the support page, and attach it to the outer box.

## How long does a refund take?

Refunds are issued within 14 days of the returned unit arriving at the
warehouse.

## Is the AR-1 Pro covered by the same warranty?

Yes, the same 24 months on the robot and dock. The Pro's water tank and mop
pad are treated as consumables and are not covered.
