# Northwind Robotics AR-1 — Quick start

The AR-1 is a robot vacuum for hard floors and low-pile carpet. This page gets
a new unit running in about fifteen minutes.

## What is in the box?

One AR-1 robot, one charging dock, one power adapter, one spare side brush
(NW-BRUSH-11), one washable filter (NW-FILT-02) and a printed safety card. The
mopping pad is not included with the base AR-1; it ships with the AR-1 Pro.

## Where should I put the charging dock?

Put the dock against a wall on a hard, level surface, with 0.5 m of clear space
on each side and 1.2 m in front. The AR-1 uses the dock's infrared beacon to
find its way home, so anything that blocks the beacon — a chair leg, a curtain,
a laundry basket — will cause a failed return and an E05 error.

## How do I charge the AR-1 for the first time?

Place the robot on the dock so both metal contacts touch. The status ring
pulses amber while charging and turns solid white when the pack is full. A
first charge from empty takes about 240 minutes. Charge the unit fully before
the first clean; the battery gauge does not calibrate until it has seen one
full charge cycle.

## How do I start the first clean?

Press and release the round button on top of the robot. The AR-1 leaves the
dock, drives the perimeter of the room, then fills in the interior in parallel
rows. The first run is slower than later runs because the robot is building its
map.

## Do I need the app to use the AR-1?

No. The button on the robot starts and stops a clean, and the dock charges it.
The app adds scheduling, no-go zones, the map view and firmware updates.
