Northwind Robotics AR-1 — safety notes

Read before first use.

The AR-1 is for indoor domestic use on dry floors only. It is not rated for
outdoor use, wet rooms, workshops or construction dust.

Do not run the AR-1 over liquid spills, pet mess, lit candles, fireplaces or
loose cables. Liquid ingress is not covered by the warranty and a cable pulled
into the roller can bring down whatever it was attached to.

Do not use the AR-1 to pick up plaster dust, toner, ash or anything smouldering.
Fine dust passes the filter and coats the fan bearing; embers are a fire risk.

Keep fingers and hair away from the main roller when the bin cover is off. The
roller can start if the button is pressed.

Use only the supplied dock and adapter. A third-party adapter with the same
plug can still deliver the wrong voltage and damage the charge circuit.

Charge and store the AR-1 between 5 and 40 degrees celsius. The robot pauses
charging outside that range and reports E08.

If the battery pack swells, deforms, or smells, stop using the robot, do not
charge it, and contact support for disposal instructions. Do not put a lithium
pack in household waste.

Children and pets should be supervised around a running robot. The AR-1 stops
on contact but it does not identify what it has touched.
