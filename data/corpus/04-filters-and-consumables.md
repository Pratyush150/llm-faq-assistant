# Filters, brushes and other consumables

## Which filter does the AR-1 use?

Part number NW-FILT-02, a washable pleated filter. One is fitted and one spare
ships in the box. The same filter fits the AR-1 and the AR-1 Pro.

## How often should I replace the filter NW-FILT-02?

Rinse it under cold water every two weeks and let it dry for 24 hours before
refitting. Replace it every six months, or sooner if the pleats stay grey after
rinsing. Never put a wet filter back in the robot; a damp filter starves the
fan and triggers an E03 airflow error.

## How do I change the main roller?

Press the two orange tabs on the roller cover, lift the cover off, and pull the
roller out from the tabbed end first. The main roller is part number
NW-ROLL-07. Cut away wrapped hair with the blade on the underside of the dust
bin lid before refitting.

## How often should I change the side brush?

The side brush, part number NW-BRUSH-11, lasts about six months. Replace it
when the bristles stay bent after you flatten them by hand — a splayed side
brush flicks debris away from the intake instead of into it.

## Does the AR-1 use disposable bags?

No. The AR-1 has a washable 0.55 litre bin. The optional auto-empty dock uses
bags, part number NW-BAG-05, and each bag holds roughly 30 bin loads.

## Where do I buy replacement parts?

From the Northwind Robotics parts page or any authorised reseller. Quote the
part number, not the model name: several parts are shared between the AR-1 and
the AR-1 Pro and several are not.
