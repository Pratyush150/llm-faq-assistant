# Wi-Fi and the app

## Which Wi-Fi networks does the AR-1 support?

2.4 GHz 802.11 b/g/n only. The AR-1 does not join 5 GHz networks. Most routers
publish both bands under one name, and the robot will simply fail to see the
network; split the bands or create a 2.4 GHz guest network for setup.

## How do I connect the AR-1 to Wi-Fi?

Hold the button and the dock button together for five seconds until the status
ring flashes blue. Open the app, choose Add device, and follow the prompts. The
phone must be on the 2.4 GHz network during setup.

## Why does the app say the robot is offline when it is running?

The robot keeps cleaning without a network connection, so "offline" during a
clean usually means weak Wi-Fi in the room it is currently in, not a fault. The
map syncs when it returns to the dock.

## Can I use the AR-1 without an account?

Yes, as a button-operated vacuum. Scheduling, zones, map history and firmware
updates all require an account, because they are stored against it.

## How do I update firmware?

The app prompts when an update is available. Keep the robot docked during the
update; it takes about 10 minutes and the status ring pulses blue. Do not
power-cycle the dock while the ring is pulsing blue.

## Does the AR-1 work with voice assistants?

It exposes start, stop and return-to-dock over the standard smart home
integrations. Room-specific commands require the map to have named rooms.
