# Error codes

The AR-1 speaks the error number and flashes the status ring red. Codes are
listed in numeric order.

## E01 — main roller stalled

Something is wrapped around the roller, or the roller cover is not seated.
Remove the roller, cut away hair with the blade under the bin lid, refit until
both orange tabs click.

## E02 — side brush stalled

Usually a rug fringe or a cable tie. Unscrew the side brush, clear it, refit.
If E02 repeats on carpet with no visible obstruction, the brush motor bearing
is failing and the unit needs service.

## E03 — airflow blocked

The filter is wet, clogged, or missing, or the bin is full. Empty the bin,
check that NW-FILT-02 is dry and correctly seated, and check the intake slot
under the robot for a stuck sock.

## E04 — cliff sensor triggered

The robot sees a drop where there is none. Dark, high-gloss floors reflect the
cliff sensor's beam poorly and read as a stair edge. Wipe the four sensor
windows on the underside. If it only happens on one dark rug, that rug is the
cause.

## E05 — cannot find the dock

The infrared beacon is blocked or the dock has no power. Check the dock LED,
clear 1.2 m in front and 0.5 m either side, and make sure the robot did not end
its run behind a closed door.

## E06 — wheel stuck

A drive wheel is not turning. Lift the robot and spin both wheels by hand. Hair
wrapped on the axle is the usual cause.

## E07 — lidar turret blocked

The turret is not spinning freely. Check for hair around the base of the turret
and confirm nothing is resting on top of the robot.

## E08 — battery too hot or too cold

Charging is paused outside 5 to 40 degrees celsius. Move the dock out of direct
sun or out of an unheated garage and wait for the pack to reach room
temperature.

## E09 — bin not detected

The dust bin is out, or its latch magnet is dirty. Reseat the bin until it
clicks.
